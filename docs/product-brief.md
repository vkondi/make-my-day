# Make My Day

A tiny web experience that gives users an unexpectedly specific prediction about their day.

The user lands on the page and sees one primary action: MAKE MY DAY.

Clicking it produces a short, funny, oddly relatable prediction.

The experience should feel playful, surprising and slightly magical.

It should take less than 10 seconds to understand.

No login. No dashboard. No unnecessary features.

The visual experience and micro-interactions are more important than technical complexity.

The result should be something people want to screenshot/share.

Detailed UX and visual system guidance lives in [design-system.md](design-system.md).
